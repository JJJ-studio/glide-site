Glide

JJJ STUDIO · Ice slide puzzle · iOS / Android

Swipe and the ball slides until something stops it. Stop it on the green ring to clear the level.

There is no sign-up. If your device is signed in to iCloud or Google Play Games, progress is saved there automatically, so you can pick up where you left off after reinstalling. Glide has ads, and the only in-app purchase is Remove Ads.

Support

Email support@studiojjj.com with your app version, device and what happened on which screen. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Restoring purchases

Remove Ads comes back with the same store account. On iPhone and iPad, tap Restore Purchases in the shop or in Settings. On Android it is restored when you open the app. Levels, coins and skins come back through cloud save (iCloud or Google Play Games). The save status is shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

Refunds

Google Play purchases follow the Google Play refund policy. App Store purchases follow Apple's refund process.
