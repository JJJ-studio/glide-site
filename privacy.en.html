Glide Privacy Policy - pre-release draft

Written: 2026-11-09

This is a draft under review before release. The effective date will be the public release date and will be shown on this screen and the web page.

1. Operator and contact

JJJ STUDIO provides Glide, a puzzle game about sliding on ice.

Privacy contact: 강성주 (Kang Seongju)

Privacy and support: support@studiojjj.com

2. Information the game handles

Glide has no sign-up, no login screen and no game server. JJJ STUDIO does not receive your name, email address, phone number or location through the game.

  • Game progress: levels, stars, coins, skins, settings and purchase status are stored on this device and are not sent to JJJ STUDIO.
  • Cloud save: if your iPhone or iPad is signed in to iCloud, or if on Android you answered 13 or older to the age question and are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress (levels, stars, coins, skins, chests, daily puzzle record and Remove Ads status) is saved to your own iCloud or Google Play Games account so you can continue after reinstalling or changing devices. Apple or Google stores it under its own privacy policy; JJJ STUDIO cannot see it. Ad age range and sound settings are not uploaded. Otherwise progress stays on this device only and is deleted with the app. Remove Ads can also be restored from your store purchase history. If Android device backup is on, the operating system may include game progress in your own Google account backup, which JJJ STUDIO cannot see.
  • Age range: the age range we ask for once when you first open the game (under 13 / 13 to 17 / 18 or older) is stored only on your device. We do not ask for your date of birth. Choosing an age range is not privacy consent.
  • Support: if you email us, we use your address and message to reply. Please do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

3. Advertising and automatic collection

Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads deliver and measure ads. Both are on Google Play's list of Families self-certified ads SDKs. The ad SDK is initialized after the age question shown on first launch; users who do not answer are treated as children and get the same protected ad requests. After that, these providers may process your IP address, App Set ID, device and operating system information, and ad impression, viewing, interaction and error information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Every user gets child-protection settings regardless of age. We removed the Android advertising ID permission and set advertising ID opt-out, child-directed treatment and non-personalized ads before the SDK starts. We do not show personalized ads or track you across apps.

How to limit it: rewarded ads only play when you choose them. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between levels. If the device is offline, no ad requests are sent. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's ad settings.

Unity player privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Unity subprocessors: https://unity.com/legal/subprocessors

4. Payments

Google Play processes Android payments and the Apple App Store processes iOS payments. The only product we sell is Remove Ads. The game does not request or store card or bank details. Transaction numbers are stored on your device to deliver the purchase and prevent duplicate grants.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Sharing, processors and international transfer

JJJ STUDIO does not share, sell or outsource the processing of your personal information. When you use ads, payments or cloud save, your device communicates directly with the companies below, and information may be processed outside your country.

  • Unity Technologies Inc. and affiliates (United States and others): network transfer when ads are requested / items in section 3 / ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention / retention per Unity's policy
  • Google LLC (United States and others): network transfer for Android payments and Google Play Games cloud save / transaction information, progress copy / payment processing, storage / retention per Google's policy and your account settings
  • Apple Inc. (United States and others): network transfer for iOS payments and iCloud cloud save / transaction information, progress copy / payment processing, storage / retention per Apple's policy and your account settings

How to refuse and what happens: if the device is offline, nothing is sent for ads; if you do not buy, nothing is sent for payments; if you sign out of iCloud or Google Play Games, nothing is sent for cloud save. Only that feature becomes unavailable and you can keep playing. Questions go to the email above.

6. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete the app. A cloud save copy stays in your iCloud or Google Play Games account until you delete it there (iCloud settings, or the Google Play Games app or account settings). Support emails are deleted within one year after the request is handled, unless the law requires us to keep them for longer. Information held by third parties follows their own policies and rights procedures.

7. Security

JJJ STUDIO runs no game server or user database. Game progress is kept in storage the operating system isolates for each app, and ads, payments and cloud save use each provider's encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Support email is handled in an account only the responsible staff can access.

8. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing by email. The game keeps no identifying information on a server of ours, so you can delete your progress yourself by deleting the app and its cloud save copy. A parent or guardian can make the same requests for a child.

9. Children

Glide is a game for all ages, including children. JJJ STUDIO does not directly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and does not ask children under 14 for personal information. Ads are requested for every user with the child-protection settings in section 3, and Android users who answered under 13, or did not answer, are not connected to Google Play Games. Parents can contact us at the email above about a child's information or to request deletion.

10. Users outside Korea

United States: under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), as described in section 9, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 and do not show behavioral advertising. Parents can contact us at the email above.

EEA and UK: progress is saved to provide the game you asked for, and ads are processed in non-personalized form only, based on our legitimate interest in running a free game. You have the right to access, delete and object, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Changes and remedies

We post changes to this policy with the effective date and announce important changes in the game beforehand.

Korea privacy complaints: Personal Information Infringement Report Center privacy.kisa.or.kr (118), Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee kopico.go.kr (1833-6972)
