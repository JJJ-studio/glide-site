Glide Terms of Use - pre-release draft

Written: 2026-11-09

This is a draft under review before release. The effective date and any changes will be announced once it is final.

1. Purpose and operator

These terms set the conditions for using Glide, provided by JJJ STUDIO. Support and disputes: support@studiojjj.com.

2. Using the service

Glide needs no account. Progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, or on Android you answered 13 or older and are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is saved there automatically and restored when you reinstall; otherwise it cannot be restored after you delete the app or change devices. Remove Ads can be restored from your store purchase history.

3. Virtual items and paid products

Coins and skins are for use inside the game and cannot be exchanged for cash or property. This does not limit any refund right you have by law.

The only paid product is Remove Ads, which stops the ads between levels. Coins are not sold. Treasure chests are free rewards earned by playing and cannot be bought. Product types, quantities and prices follow the purchase screen and the store payment screen (Google Play or Apple App Store).

4. Withdrawal, cancellation and refunds

You may have the right to withdraw from a purchase as provided by the law that applies to you. For Korean consumers this is generally within 7 days, subject to the legal exceptions for digital content.

Purchases made by a minor without a guardian's consent may be cancelled as provided by law. Parents can require approval for a child's purchases with Google Play Family Link or Apple's Ask to Buy.

Google Play purchases can be refunded through Google Play, and App Store purchases through Apple's Report a Problem page, or contact us by email. Please do not send card numbers or passwords.

Google Play refunds: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

Apple App Store refunds: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223

5. Advertising and third-party services

Rewarded ad rewards are granted according to the viewing conditions shown on screen. Ads may be unavailable because of network or inventory limits. Remove Ads removes interstitial ads between levels; you can still choose to watch rewarded ads. Third-party services are also subject to their own terms.

6. Prohibited conduct

Do not tamper with the game, manipulate data, abuse payments or infringe the rights of others.

7. Changes and closure

If the service closes, we will give reasonable notice of the schedule and of how unused paid content will be refunded or compensated, in line with applicable law.

8. Changes, disputes and statutory rights

We will announce the effective date and content of any final or changed terms in the game. Mandatory law and your statutory consumer rights take precedence over this document.
